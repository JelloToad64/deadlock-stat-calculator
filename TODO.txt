PART D: The items

the hardest part.

step 0: Inventory.

A hero can only have 12 items max in their inventory.

step 1: component building.

some items are on a discount if a different item was bought before hand. If a multiple items get a discount from a single item, only one item can redeem the discount, the rest are back to normal price. buying a component before buying its upgrade lets you keep the component if the upgraded item is sold. buying an item on discount also does not take up a new slot, it will replace the slot of the item it upgrades from. 

if you directly buy and item that has a unpurchased component, the component is bought, it is also sold if the item is sold.
a component will transfer what item it upgrades too if the item is was upgraded was sold, and another that would have upgraded from is also bought.
